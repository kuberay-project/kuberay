# KubeRay Governance

## Roles

There are 5 roles in the KubeRay community:

* Contributor: A contributor is a member who contributes to the KubeRay project through code, documentation,
  PR reviews, and answering questions.
* Triager: A triager has the permission to triage issues and PRs.
* Reviewer: A reviewer can approve PRs.
* Committer: A committer can merge PRs and is responsible for the KubeRay project.
* Technical steering committee members: This group drives the overall project direction.

This document describes the mechanisms of governance. For guidelines for how to participate, please take a look at COMMUNITY.md.

### Triagers

Any member of the TSC can appoint a triager directly, so no vote is required.

### Reviewers

Reviewers are nominated by any member of the TSC. They are approved if there are no objections from other TSC
members within one week, or be approved by a supermajority (greater than 67%) vote of the TSC if an objection
is raised. The TSC votes on reviewer nominations once per KubeRay minor release.

Reviewers are automatically moved to emeritus status after 12 months of inactivity. Reactivation is by a TSC vote.

### Committers

Committers are added by a supermajority (greater than 67%) of the TSC and removed by a majority vote
(greater than 50%) of the TSC. The TSC votes on committer nominations once per KubeRay minor release.

Committers are automatically moved to emeritus status after 12 months with no merged pull requests authored
or reviewed. Reactivation is by a TSC vote.

### Technical Steering Committee

The technical steering committee (the "TSC") is responsible for all technical oversight of KubeRay.

The TSC can have up to 5 members. This ceiling can be raised by a supermajority (greater than 67%) of the TSC.

Any member of the TSC can nominate someone to become a member of the TSC. The addition or removal of a TSC
member requires a supermajority (greater than 67%) of the entire TSC.

TSC members are expected to serve at least 1 year with active participation in TSC meetings.

### Amendments

Changes to this governance document require approval from a supermajority (greater than 67%) of TSC and
should be discussed in a TSC meeting before merging.
